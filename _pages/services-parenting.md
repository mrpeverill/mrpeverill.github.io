Parenting a child with disruptive behavior - defiance, aggression, difficulty following rules, tantrums, and outbursts -- can be exhausting, consuming, and embarassing. Parents of children with these concerns are inundated with advice from family, friends, schools, and social media, but may still end up feeling like they're walking on egg shells around their own children. Too often, advice fails because it was intended for average, neurotypical children -- **it was not written for your child**. Whether the problem is ultimately rooted in ADHD, autism, anxiety, or an atypical temperament, I can help you design an individualized set of precision parenting techniques that is adapted to **your child's** unique psychology. Evidence-based behavioral approaches are highly effective for these concerns, and research consistently shows that working with parents is often the most powerful lever available.

## Who this is for

I work with families of children and adolescents experiencing:

- Oppositional defiant disorder (ODD) and conduct problems
- ADHD-related behavioral challenges
- Frequent tantrums, defiance, or difficulty following rules
- Aggression or explosive behavior at home or school
- Difficulty with transitions, compliance, or peer relationships
- Avoidant anxiety

Especially for younger children, the most effective approach is often to work primarily — or entirely — with parents rather than the child directly. Changing in therapy is hard, and children do not always have the self-awareness, executive functioning, and motivation to benefit on their own -- they may not have a problem that they want to solve. For that reason, parents are the often the most powerful agents of change in a young child's environment.

## How it works

Treatment draws on evidence-based strategies, including parent management training and SPACE (Supportive Parenting for Anxious Childhood Emotions), with strong research support. Sessions focus on helping parents understand the principles driving their child's behavior, develop effective and consistent responses, and build a stronger relationship alongside clearer expectations.
